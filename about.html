About Us Page

Sisonke Auto Care is a leading automotive service provider dedicated to delivering exceptional quality services to our customers.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, affordable, professional vehicle maintenance services, engine diagnostics and repair, brake repairs and replacement, suspension and steering repairs, oil and filter changes, battery testing and replacement, tire fitting, balancing and puncture repairs, wheel alignment, auto electrical repairs etc., while ensuring customer satisfaction and vehicle safety.

Our Vision

To become a trusted automative service provider in the community by expanding its digital presence, such as building a professional business website which will provide a good google business profile, online service catalogue and pricing, online appointment booking, search engine optimization and email marketing. To improve customer accessibility, provide a click-to-call contact option, google map location and directions, mobile mechanic services, SMS or email reminders, and clear contact information.

Target Audience

The website will target vehicle owners, young professionals, families, and businesses with cars that require reliable vehicle maintenance services.
